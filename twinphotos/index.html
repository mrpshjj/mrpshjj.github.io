Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos safe?

Yes. Photos you delete with Twin Photos are moved to the iOS Recently Deleted album, where they stay for 30 days. You can restore them anytime from the Photos app: Albums → Recently Deleted.

Why didn't my storage free up right after deleting?

iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To free up space immediately, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All.

Does Twin Photos upload my photos?

No. All analysis runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded and never leave your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy.

The app says it needs full photo access. Why?

Twin Photos compares photos across your whole library to find duplicates, so it needs Full Access. You can grant it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Twin Photos → Full Access.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Twin Photos → Cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the current period ends.
