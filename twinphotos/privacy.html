Twin Photos — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

Twin Photos: Duplicate Cleaner ("the app") is built to protect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it never touches.

Your photos never leave your device

All photo analysis — finding duplicates, grouping similar photos, and recommending best shots — happens entirely on your iPhone. The app does not upload your photos, thumbnails, or any information derived from them (such as image hashes) to any server. We do not operate a server that receives photo data.

What the app stores on your device

  • Analysis cache: image fingerprints used to speed up repeat scans. Stored only in the app's local database on your device.
  • Free usage counter: how many photos you have cleaned with the free tier, stored in the device Keychain. It contains no personal information.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes your purchase receipt and a randomly generated anonymous identifier. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or photos. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

Anonymous usage analytics

To see where the app is confusing or failing people, the app sends a small set of anonymous usage events to PostHog. Each event records which step of the app was reached, plus simple numbers about that step — for example that a scan finished, how long it took, how many duplicates were found, that the subscription screen appeared, or which plan was tapped.

Your photos are never part of this. No photo, thumbnail, image fingerprint, file name, album name, or capture date is ever included in an analytics event. Everything the app works out about your library stays in the local database on your device.

Alongside those events, PostHog records standard technical information: app launches, device model, iOS version, app version, language, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. Events are grouped under the same randomly generated anonymous identifier used for purchases, so returning use can be recognised without knowing who you are. Because the app has no accounts and never collects a name, email, or phone number, this data cannot be traced back to you. See the PostHog Privacy Policy for details.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising SDKs
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), and no tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites
  • No accounts — the app never asks for your name, email, or phone number
  • No selling of personal data, and no sharing with data brokers

Photo library access

The app requests photo library access solely to find and clean duplicate photos at your request. Deleted photos are moved to the iOS "Recently Deleted" album by the system, where they remain recoverable for 30 days.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The anonymous usage events described above contain no personal information.
