Twin Photos — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Twin Photos: Duplicate Cleaner ("the app") is built to protect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it never touches.

Your photos never leave your device

All photo analysis — finding duplicates, grouping similar photos, and recommending best shots — happens entirely on your iPhone. The app does not upload your photos, thumbnails, or any information derived from them (such as image hashes) to any server. We do not operate a server that receives photo data.

What the app stores on your device

  • Analysis cache: image fingerprints used to speed up repeat scans. Stored only in the app's local database on your device.
  • Free usage counter: how many photos you have cleaned with the free tier, stored in the device Keychain. It contains no personal information.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, which processes your purchase receipt and a randomly generated anonymous identifier. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or photos. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy for details.

What we do not do

  • No ads and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No accounts — the app never asks for your name, email, or phone number
  • No selling or sharing of personal data

Photo library access

The app requests photo library access solely to find and clean duplicate photos at your request. Deleted photos are moved to the iOS "Recently Deleted" album by the system, where they remain recoverable for 30 days.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
